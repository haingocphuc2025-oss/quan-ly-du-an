# AGENT RULES - AUTO LOOP

1. Nguoi dung chi can giao muc tieu; khong bat duyet spec hoac task trung gian.
2. Doc `00_TONG_HOP/README_FIRST.md` va trang thai hien tai truoc.
3. Xac minh baseline trong `02_SOURCE/TRANG_THAI.md` truoc khi sua.
4. Truy vet `SPEC -> TASK -> BAN GIAO -> REPORT -> BASELINE`.
5. SPEC chi sua `01_SPEC/NEW/`; THI CONG khong sua spec trong cung luot code.
6. Staging: `02_SOURCE/02_SAN_PHAM_DON_FILE/STAGING/`.
7. Ban giao: `02_SOURCE/03_BAN_GIAO/`; bang chung: `04_REPORTS/`.
8. Khong dung `02_SOURCE/99_LUU_TRU_CU/` lam chi dao.
9. Khong ha acceptance criteria hoac tat test de che loi.
10. Chi DONE sau review, verify, regression, nang baseline va dong bo spec.
11. Toi da 5 vong; sau do BLOCKED.
12. Chi hoi khi thieu quyet dinh nghiep vu, can quyen, co nguy co mat du lieu hoac BLOCKED.
13. Khong doc toan bo repo/HTML/log theo mac dinh. Nap ngu canh toi thieu theo thu tu: trang thai va ban giao hien tai -> spec lien quan -> knowledge graph/module lien quan -> test lien quan.
14. Uu tien knowledge graph, diff va tim kiem muc tieu; chi mo rong file/pham vi khi co bang chung anh huong.
15. Truoc khi code, neu co tu hai cach hieu lam thay doi hanh vi, du lieu, format, mapping, acceptance criteria hoac pham vi, hoi lai mot cau ngan gon. Day duoc xem la thieu quyet dinh nghiep vu theo muc 12.
16. Moi cau hoi lam ro phai gom: cach hieu hien tai, quyet dinh con thieu va cac lua chon cu the. Khong hoi dieu co the tu xac minh trong spec, code, test hoac report.
17. Khi tiep tuc phien ban/tinh nang, doc ho so ban giao va diff truoc; sau khi ban giao phai cap nhat trang thai/report de agent sau khong nghien cuu lai tu dau.
