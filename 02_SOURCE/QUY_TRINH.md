# QUY TRINH HAI VAI TRO

## 1. SPEC tao viec

- Tao spec trong `../01_SPEC/NEW/`.
- Ghi muc tieu, pham vi, tieu chi chap nhan, test case, baseline va output du kien.
- Nguoi dung chon spec NEW can thi cong.

## 2. THI CONG thuc hien

- Doc dung spec duoc giao va baseline hien hanh trong `TRANG_THAI.md`.
- Tao output moi trong `02_SAN_PHAM_DON_FILE/STAGING`.
- Chi thay doi trong pham vi spec; neu spec thieu hoac mau thuan thi tra lai SPEC lam ro.
- Chay kiem tra cu phap/build, test case trong spec, hoi quy va console trinh duyet.

## 3. THI CONG ban giao

- Lap bien ban theo `03_BAN_GIAO/MAU_BAN_GIAO.md`.
- Ghi ro staging, thay doi, ket qua test, loi con lai va yeu cau deploy.
- Spec van o `NEW` trong thoi gian cho nghiem thu.

## 4. SPEC nghiem thu

- Test doc lap theo tieu chi chap nhan.
- Neu khong dat: ghi cach tai hien va tra lai THI CONG sua; spec van o NEW.
- Neu dat: xac nhan bien ban va cho phep nang baseline.

## 5. Hoan tat

- THI CONG dua san pham dat vao `VERSIONS/vNN_baseline.*` va cap nhat `TRANG_THAI.md`.
- SPEC chuyen thu muc spec tu `NEW` sang `DA_TRIEN_KHAI` va cap nhat `DANH_MUC_SPEC.md`.

## Definition of Done

- Dung spec NEW duoc giao.
- Tat ca test bat buoc dat.
- Khong co loi build/console nghiem trong.
- Co ban giao va bang chung.
- SPEC nghiem thu dat.
- Baseline, trang thai phien ban va trang thai spec da cap nhat.
